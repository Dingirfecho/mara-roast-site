    GURU PRAJWAL RATNA VAJRACHARYA · TEMPLE + ONLINE

    TEMPLE + ONLINE

    Suchandra was the king who asked the Buddha for a path he could practice without abandoning his kingdom — the template for anyone practicing seriously while still running a life. No prior experience needed; texts provided.

    TEMPLE + ONLINE

    A retreat built around the Three Long-Life Deities: the view, the root sadhana, and the movements. The premise is blunt and correct — a long, healthy life isn't an indulgence, it's practice infrastructure. No prerequisites; texts provided.

    TEMPLE + ONLINE

Nritya Mandala Mahavihara

1405 SE 40TH AVE · PORTLAND, OR 97214
  • From PDX: 20–30 minutes by car; rideshare is easiest. By transit, roughly 45–60 minutes via MAX plus a bus transfer — build in extra time.

§ CITY GUIDE

If you're flying in for a few days

Where to stay

Buckman, Hawthorne, or Division/Clinton — walking distance from the temple and from the best food in the city. Skip staying near the airport.

Weather

Early October means cool, often damp. Pack layers and something waterproof — it won't be dramatic, but it won't be dry either.

Food

SE Division and Hawthorne are dense with genuinely excellent restaurants and coffee. Portland punches way above its size here.

A free afternoon

Powell's City of Books downtown, or a walk through Forest Park for tree cover and quiet before or after a day of teachings.
