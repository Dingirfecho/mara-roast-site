§ WHAT IS A MARA ROAST

Five days to outlast what's chasing you

Mara is the force in Buddhist cosmology that shows up right before a breakthrough — not as a monster, but as distraction, anxiety, and the sense that the ground keeps shifting. Mara Roast Europe spends five straight days building the opposite of that: a real refuge, a trained mind, a clear map of how suffering actually works, and — at the center of the week — a complete transmission built around the Buddha of Boundless Life.

This isn't a lecture series. It's refuge, then training, then the view, then the transmission, then the room opened up for whatever you're still sitting on.

  • Day 1 · Refuge

    Turning toward what's genuine and liberating — the inner anchor everything else in the week builds on.

  • Day 2 · Mindfulness

    The Four Foundations of Mindfulness (Satipatthana) — training awareness from the body on up.

  • Day 3 · Interdependence

    The Twelve Links of Dependent Origination — the causal chain of samsara, and where it breaks.

  • Day 4 · Empowerment

    Amitayus, Buddha of Boundless Life, from the Chikshe Kundrol cycle of the 9th Karmapa.

  • Day 5 · Open Dharma

    Visualization beyond sight, then an open Q&A — bring the question you've been sitting on.

§ CITY GUIDE

If you're flying in for the week

Where you are

Kaštela is a string of coastal towns on Croatia's Dalmatian coast, between Split's old Roman core and the UNESCO town of Trogir — both worth a half-day each if you have one.

Weather

Late October on the Adriatic is shoulder season — mild, cooling, with swimming mostly done for the year. Pack layers and something for the evenings.

Getting around

A rental car makes exploring the coast easiest; Split's old town and the ferries out to the islands are both manageable as a day trip if you arrive a day or two early.

Any confusion

Email Lama Chris directly — he's the best source for exact logistics once your dates are confirmed.
