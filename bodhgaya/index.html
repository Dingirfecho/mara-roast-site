§ WHY BODHGAYA

The one Mara Roast that's also the original

Every other Mara Roast borrows its name from a story. This one happens at the actual site of it. Bodh Gaya is where the Buddha sat beneath the Bodhi Tree, where Mara threw everything he had at him — fear, desire, doubt, an army of illusions — and where none of it worked. The Mahabodhi Temple stands there now, marking the spot.

This isn't a retreat built around a teaching schedule. It's a pilgrimage: five days in the place itself, practicing where the thing we keep naming "Mara Roast" after everywhere else actually happened once, for real, under a tree that's still there.

  • The Bodhi Tree

    A direct descendant of the tree the Buddha sat beneath — still rooted at the Mahabodhi Temple complex.

  • The Vajrasana

    The Diamond Throne marking the exact spot of the enlightenment — the literal ground this whole tradition stands on.

  • Kora

    Circumambulating the temple in the company of pilgrims from every Buddhist country and lineage on earth.

  • Living Lineage

    Led in person by Lama Cele, on the ground where the lineage itself began.

Bodh Gaya, Bihar, India

NEAR THE MAHABODHI TEMPLE · BODH GAYA, BIHAR, INDIA
  • Nearest airport: Gaya Airport (GAY) is the closest, about 10–15 km from Bodh Gaya — it has limited international connections, mostly seasonal. Patna (PAT), roughly 2.5–3 hours by road, is a more reliable year-round option for international travelers.

§ CITY GUIDE

If you're flying in for the week

Where you are

Bodh Gaya is one of Buddhism's four great pilgrimage sites, a UNESCO World Heritage complex, with monasteries built by Buddhist communities from Thailand, Japan, Tibet, Bhutan, and beyond all within walking distance of each other.

Weather

Late January to early February is cool-season Bihar — pleasant, mild days and genuinely cold mornings and nights. Pack layers.

Nearby pilgrimage sites

Rajgir and the ruins of Nalanda, once the great Buddhist university, are both feasible day trips if you have extra time before or after.

Any confusion

Message Vajrakula directly — logistics in Bodh Gaya are easiest to sort out with someone who's coordinating the group.
